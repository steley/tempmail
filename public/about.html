About TempMail.cc

Why we built it

Your real email address is valuable — and every sign-up form on the internet wants it. Hand it out, and sooner or later it ends up on a marketing list, in a data breach, or buried under daily newsletters you never asked for.

TempMail.cc is a free, self-hosted temporary email service that gives you a disposable, receive-only inbox the moment you open the page. Use it for sign-ups, downloads, testing, or anywhere you don't want to give away your real address. No registration, no password, no personal data.

How it works

  • A disposable address is generated instantly and works in real time — emails arrive as they are sent;
  • All messages are automatically and permanently deleted when you reload the page or your session expires;
  • It is receive-only: sending is not supported, and attachments are disabled;
  • Email content is rendered in a sandboxed environment, so messages cannot run scripts on the site.

Our principles

  • Collect as little as possible. No accounts, no tracking profiles, no data selling — see our Privacy Policy;
  • Keep it fast and simple. One page, one inbox, zero friction;
  • Be honest about the limits. Temporary inboxes are public by nature — never use them for anything confidential.
