What's New

Notable changes to TempMail.cc, newest first. Small fixes are not listed.

October 21, 2026

  • NewGuides by use case. Facebook, Instagram, Discord, Steam, Temu, and what a 30-minute window actually covers.
  • NewA clearer About page. Who runs the site, how the mail path works, and which limits are deliberate.
  • NewFAQ rewritten around the questions people actually ask — lifetime, attachments, why there is no password.

October 19, 2026

  • NewDelete a message yourself instead of waiting for it to expire.
  • For self-hostersStricter defaults. The web port binds to loopback; the per-IP mail limit is configurable.

October 16, 2026

  • NewRecovery links. Copy a private link to reopen your inbox — with its mail — on another device within 30 minutes. Treat it like a password.
  • NewAn empty inbox now explains itself, including the fact that many sites block disposable domains on their side.

October 15, 2026

  • NewInstallable as an app — add it to your home screen.
  • NewError codes instead of a generic failure message.
  • PerfPages are cached at the CDN edge, and the inbox connects over WebSocket first.

October 14, 2026

  • NewDark mode, and a QR code for opening your address on another device.
  • FixReliable reconnection after an interrupted connection.

October 13, 2026

  • NewFAQ page and a Docker guide for running your own instance.
  • SecurityStrict security headers, per-mailbox rate limiting, and a full audit pass.

Complete rewrite — October 2026

  • A full rewrite of the 2015-era forsaken-mail project: real-time inbox, no registration, no password, source on GitHub.
