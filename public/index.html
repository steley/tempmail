    What TempMail.cc does

    TempMail.cc provides a secure, anonymous, temporary email address that protects your personal inbox from spam, phishing, and unwanted marketing emails. No registration, no password, no personal data — your disposable address is ready the moment this page loads.

    • Emails are kept for about 30 minutes, then automatically and permanently deleted.
    • Only text and HTML messages are supported. Attachments are disabled.
    • This is a receive-only mailbox; sending emails is not available.

    What is a temporary email address?

    A temporary (disposable) email address is a short-lived inbox that you can use instead of your real email when signing up for services, downloading files, or testing applications. It protects your personal inbox from spam, phishing, and unwanted marketing — and from services that sell your address to advertisers.

    Is TempMail.cc free?

    Yes, TempMail.cc is completely free. No registration, password, or personal information is required — a disposable address is generated the moment you open the page, and you can change it or set a custom one at any time.

    How long do emails stay?

    Emails arrive in real time and are kept for about 30 minutes after the last message, then automatically and permanently deleted. Reopen the site in the same browser and your inbox comes back with its recent mail, or copy a recovery link to reopen it on another device within that window. Because temporary inboxes are public by nature, anyone who knows the address can read it — never use TempMail.cc for confidential communications or important account recovery.
